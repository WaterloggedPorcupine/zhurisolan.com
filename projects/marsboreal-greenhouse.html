Marsboreal Greenhouse

A response to NASA's BIG Idea Challenge for the design and operation of a Mars greenhouse.

Overview

The 2019 BIG Idea Challenge presented the opportunity to design, develop, and demonstrate an idea for the installation and sustainable operation of a habitat-sized Mars greenhouse of which the primary purpose was food production.

Not only did we compete in the NASA BIG Idea Challenge, we also presented our poster at the 2019 International Conference on Environmental Systems.
