MorningBoard

A personal productivity dashboard for Windows — built with Electron.

Desktop App Windows Electron

Overview

MorningBoard is a personal dashboard I built because the tools I was using every morning — calendar, to-do lists, news, reading trackers — were all separate apps with separate windows. I wanted one place that opened with my computer and had everything I needed to start the day.

The app uses a multi-tab layout so each context gets its own space without cluttering the others. Light/dark mode switches automatically based on the time of day.

Key Features

Google Calendar Integration Pull in events directly from your Google account. View the week at a glance from the Calendar tab.
Books Tracker Log books with reading status and ratings. Lightweight — no Goodreads account required.
Fanfiction Import Paste a fic URL or metadata to add it to your reading list. Predates the AO3 extension.
Auto Light/Dark Mode Switches color scheme based on time of day — light in the morning, dark after sunset.
News Feed Curated RSS feed reader pulled into the News Feed tab. One less browser tab to manage.
Job Search Tab Dedicated space for tracking job applications, links, and notes during an active search.

Design

Designed the full color scheme and typography from scratch. The palette is calm enough to look at first thing in the morning without being sterile.
